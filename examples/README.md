# Examples

This directory contains illustrative examples only.

Examples are non-normative, are not complete implementations, and must not be treated as compliance templates, operational authorizations, institutional approvals, or mandatory forms.

They show how domain profiles may express the Governance Constitution’s concepts through practical artifacts.

## Available examples

* `legal_lba_example.json` — illustrative jurisdiction-neutral legal legal Legitimacy Binding Artifact.
* `agentic_pr_lba_example.json` — illustrative environment-neutral Legitimacy Binding Artifact for scoped agentic pull-request preparation.

## Validation note

Structural conformity with a JSON Schema does not by itself establish constitutional legitimacy, legal validity, safety, correctness, authorization, or institutional approval.

Examples must be evaluated against the applicable Constitution version, mMRM specification, schema, policy snapshot, lane schema, domain profile, and local institutional requirements.
